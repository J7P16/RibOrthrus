ORTHRUS: TOWARD EVOLUTIONARY AND FUNCTIONAL RNA FOUNDATIONAL MODELS
===================================================================

Abstract:
---------
- Heavy limitations on predicting key RNA properties, even in the face
    of rapidly accumulating genomic data
- There are many pretrained genomic foundation models BUT they are trained
    using strategies borrowed from textual domains that DO NOT
    leverage biological domain knowledge.
- In response to this issue, Orthrus is created as an RNA foundation model 
    (pretrained using a self-supervised contrastive learning objective 
    with biological augmentations)
- Orthrus maximizes embedding similarity between pairs of RNA transcripts from
    orthologous genes in 400+ mammalian species.
- Orthrus' representations outperform other foundation models on diff tasks and
    requires ONLY A FRACTION of fine-tuning data.
